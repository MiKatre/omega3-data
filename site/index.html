How to read it

€ / g is the pack price divided by the EPA+DHA in the pack, from the label. € / month is the cost of your daily target for 30 days; units / day is how many capsules (or ml of oil) that takes. Each row is one product; "Details" lists every shop selling it.

Freshness and lab data

TOTOX measures oxidation; lower is fresher. IFOS passes oils at ≤ 19.5, the industry body GOED allows ≤ 26. Lab values come from the latest IFOS lot report matching the product's label. Claim values are the brand's statement.

How the data is made

Prices are collected automatically from shop pages and feeds. EPA/DHA amounts are read from each listing's label text; "Details" shows the exact text used. Shipping and promotions are not included. This compares value and published test data; it is not dosing advice.
